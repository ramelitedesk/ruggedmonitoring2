Transformers

From power generation to data centers, RM EYE uses advanced algorithms to track bushing capacitance, partial discharge and winding hotspot temperature — analyzing trends, catching anomalies and predicting failure before it happens.

The result: a more reliable infrastructure, optimized asset performance and maximized grid stability.

Batteries

Dependable energy storage keeps power stable and uninterrupted. RM EYE tracks temperature, state of charge and internal resistance in real time to prevent premature failure and extend battery life across industrial, renewable and backup applications.

A proactive approach that safeguards reliability and supports sustainable operations.

Power Cables

As cables age, hidden defects raise the risk of catastrophic failure. RM EYE detects thermal stress, partial discharge activity and insulation degradation before they cause outages — with dashboards and customizable alerts driving predictive maintenance.

Better grid stability, lower maintenance cost, uninterrupted operations.

  • Insulation health98%
  • Thermal stressLow
  • PD activityNone detected

Rotating Machines

Unplanned failures disrupt operations fast. RM EYE continuously monitors temperature, vibration and electrical stress, reducing risk and improving visibility through actionable insight.

Maintenance aligned to real-time status means less downtime and longer equipment life.
